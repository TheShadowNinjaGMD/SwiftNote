Built for Mac

It feels right at home.

Native Swift, Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and frosted glass everywhere else, with a Light, Dark or follow-the-system look.

Live Markdown

Bold, italic, underline, headings, lists and code appear as you type. The markers stay visible, just dimmed, so nothing is hidden from you.

Global keybinds

Open, save and dictate from any app. Every shortcut is yours to change, or switch them to app-only.

Quietly fast

Lives in the menu bar and opens instantly. Pick any SF Symbol or your own SVG as its icon.

Dictate anywhere

Apple speech, local whisper.cpp models or an OpenAI-compatible API. Filler words and spoken punctuation handled for you.

Sticky notes

Colour, pin and tag notes, then float them on your desktop. Drag them anywhere and resize them to fit.

Backed up by default

Autosaves to Application Support and mirrors to a Desktop folder that iCloud can sync. Export or import settings any time.

Say it. It's written.

Choose the engine that fits how you work. Press your shortcut, talk, and the text lands in your note.

Apple speech

Works out of the box, with on-device recognition and automatic punctuation.

whisper.cpp, local

Download a model in Settings and keep every word on your Mac.

Online API

Point it at any OpenAI-compatible transcription endpoint. Keys live in your Keychain.
